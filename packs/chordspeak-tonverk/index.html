How to load it

  1. On the Tonverk, open SETTINGS > USB DISK MODE and press YES, then connect it to your computer. A card reader works too.
  2. Copy the Chordspeak and Chordspeak_LowAlias folders from User/Wavetables in the download into User/Wavetables on the SD card. Create that folder if it isn't there.
  3. Eject the card on the computer, then press NO on the Tonverk to leave disk mode.
  4. On an audio track, choose the Wavefinder machine, pick a table with SLOT, and sweep POS to move through the waves. ANIM with A.POS animates the sweep.

The 127 files fill exactly one project's wavetable pool, so the whole set loads into an otherwise empty project.

About the pack

Chordspeak started with one idea: a chord that talks. In the twelve chord speech tables each wave holds a whole chord, so sweeping position plays a short phrase: a question rising, a call and a reply, a tight cluster opening out to an octave. Several of them are tuned in just intonation. Around those I built tables that move the way voices, wind, animals, glass and slow physical processes move, with formants sliding, a breath turning into a flute tone, and partials dying away one at a time. Every sound was generated from scratch. Nothing is sampled from a recording. Most tables also have a band-limited LowAlias twin for when the bright ones get gritty above about C4.

FAQ

Can I use these in released music?
Yes. You can use them in your own music, film, game and broadcast work, live performance and sound design, including commercial releases, without payment or credit.
What can't I do?
Redistribute, resell, share or upload the wavetable files themselves, alone or inside another pack or instrument, modified or not.
Which Tonverk OS do I need?
OS 1.3.0 or later, which added the Wavefinder machine.
When should I use the LowAlias tables?
When a bright table sounds gritty in the upper register, roughly C4 and above.
